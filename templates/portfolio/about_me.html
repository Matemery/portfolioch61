My role as a dev

I'm a full-stack developer who enjoys owning a project end-to-end — from the first wireframe to the server that keeps it running at 2am. I like the front end because it's where design decisions become real, tangible experiences: getting the spacing right, making an interaction feel smooth, building something people actually want to use. I like the back end because it's where the real problem-solving happens — designing data models, building APIs that make sense, and figuring out how to make things fast and reliable as they scale. What I care about most is the connective tissue between the two: writing code that's easy to hand off, systems that don't fall over under real-world use, and products that feel as solid on the inside as they look on the outside. I try to bring the same mix of curiosity and craftsmanship to every layer of the stack.
